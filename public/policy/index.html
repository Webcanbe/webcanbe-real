Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Webcanbe collects, uses, stores, shares, retains, and protects information when you use webcanbe.com and related Webcanbe services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Webcanbe may process account information, session metadata, workspace and project source files, project history, marketplace and creator records, settings, support communications, and operational or security logs needed to provide and protect the service.

2. Google sign-in data

When you choose Google sign-in, Webcanbe requests the standard OpenID Connect scopes openid, email, and profile. Google may provide a stable Google account identifier and basic profile information such as your email address, name, and profile picture.

Webcanbe uses this information only to authenticate you, create or identify your Webcanbe account, maintain your signed-in session, and provide account-related features. This sign-in flow does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or other Google product data.

3. Google tokens and authentication

Webcanbe verifies the Google identity returned by the OpenID Connect flow and maps that verified identity to an internal Webcanbe account. The current sign-in design does not persist Google access tokens or refresh tokens for this authentication flow.

4. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate workspaces and product features, preserve project state and purchase records where applicable, secure the service, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, provide support, improve reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

5. Sharing and third-party service providers

Webcanbe does not sell Google user data and does not use Google sign-in data for advertising. Information may be processed by infrastructure, authentication, payment, deployment, analytics, or support providers only as needed to provide the feature you request, operate the service, protect users, or comply with law.

6. Storage and security

Webcanbe uses secure transport and server-side authorization boundaries for hosted account operations. Authentication credentials are verified server-side. We limit stored authentication and account-mapping data to what is reasonably needed to operate and secure the service.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, preserve legitimate project or transaction records, maintain security, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and why it is needed.

8. Deletion and user choices

You may request deletion of your Webcanbe account or associated personal information by contacting support@webcanbe.com. You can also revoke Webcanbe's Google account access from your Google Account permissions. Revoking Google access prevents future use of that authorization but does not automatically delete information already stored by Webcanbe.

9. Cookies and session data

Webcanbe may use cookies or similar browser storage that are necessary for authentication, session continuity, security, preferences, and core product functionality. We may also process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps for security and reliability purposes.

10. Children's privacy

Webcanbe does not knowingly use Google sign-in information for targeted advertising or sell personal information. Where age-related consent or restrictions apply, the service is operated subject to applicable law and platform requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Webcanbe adds or changes features. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy, Google sign-in data, or deletion requests can be sent to support@webcanbe.com.
